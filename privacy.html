Privacy Policy

Who we are

Let's Sushi is developed by TheGameIsDifficult. This policy covers the Android game, package thegameisdifficult.sushigarden, and its support and privacy website. Contact thegameisdifficult@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

Game progress and settings

The game keeps progress, coins, collections, nursery configurations, records, language and audio preferences, and a local save backup in app storage on your device. It offers no player account registration or developer-operated cloud save. The Android build disables standard app backup. We do not receive your local game save through our own server.

Age and privacy choices

In builds with Privacy settings, the game asks for an age range without selecting an answer for you. It stores the selected range or Prefer not to say on your device, rather than a date of birth. The game uses it to apply advertising and consent safeguards. Players under 18 and players who choose not to provide an age receive under-age treatment. Choosing Later keeps advertising requests disabled until an age choice is made. Google may store consent choices on the device and process consent-related information to provide its privacy forms.

Advertising and information used

Rewarded ads are optional and can accelerate growing seedlings. In builds with Privacy settings, all ad requests use child-directed treatment, a general-audience content rating limit and a non-personalized-ad request. Age-related flags are set before the advertising SDK is initialized. The game checks Google User Messaging Platform consent status before loading ads and offers Google's consent form when required. If the check fails, ad loading remains disabled until a successful retry. Ads can be preloaded after this process, before you press the watch-ad button.

Google's advertising services

Google's advertising SDKs can process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, app and ad interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and device or account identifiers. The exact data depends on the SDK version and privacy settings. Child-directed and under-age settings restrict advertising identifier use and interest-based advertising. Non-personalized advertising can still use data to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Google and its service partners process this information under their policies, possibly in countries other than yours. Google documents TLS encryption for SDK data in transit.

Earlier internal test builds

Earlier internal test builds without Privacy settings initialize the advertising SDK on entry to the game screen and may request ads before the player chooses to watch. They do not contain the age-screen and consent safeguards described above. Those safeguards take effect when a build containing Privacy settings is installed. This policy does not retroactively change data processing by earlier builds.

Children and parents

The game's intended audience includes children aged 6 and above. In builds with Privacy settings, we apply child-directed ad treatment to every player, and under-age consent treatment to players under 18 or with an unspecified age. The age range stays on the device; the game sends treatment flags to Google's services. Children can play without watching ads. Parents and guardians can contact us about privacy, correction or deletion requests. If you believe a child sent us personal information by email, contact us so we can locate and delete it where applicable.

Support and website visits

If you email us, we receive your address and message content and use them to respond to support and privacy requests. Gmail processes this correspondence. Please include only what is needed to handle the request. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process visitor IP addresses and access information for operation and security under GitHub's privacy statement. We have not added our own website analytics or advertising scripts.

Retention, security and deletion

Local game data and age preferences remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game. This erases local progress. We retain support correspondence for no more than one month after a request is resolved, unless applicable law requires longer retention. Google's advertising and consent data and GitHub's hosting records are retained under their respective policies. Deleting local game data does not delete information already processed by those providers. We restrict access to our support mailbox and use the security protections of our service providers.

Your controls and requests

You can decline rewarded ads and keep playing. In builds with Privacy settings, open Settings → Privacy settings to read this policy, update your age range, or open Ad privacy options. Where Google's privacy form is required, that option lets you review or change your choices. Where no form is required, it refreshes the privacy check. Android offers advertising ID controls where supported. You or a guardian may contact us to request access, correction, deletion or other applicable rights concerning information we hold. We may request enough information to verify and locate the request. We cannot identify a device's local save from an email address.

Policy changes

We update this page when our data practices change and display the effective date. Where required, material changes will also be communicated in the game. The privacy safeguards available on your device depend on the installed game build.

隱私權政策

開發者與適用範圍

《Let's Sushi》由 TheGameIsDifficult 開發。本政策適用於套件名稱 thegameisdifficult.sushigarden 的 Android 遊戲，以及其支援和隱私網站。隱私問題或請求可寄至 thegameisdifficult@gmail.com。

遊戲進度與設定

遊戲進度、金幣、收藏、養育配置、紀錄、語言與音效偏好，以及本機存檔備份，均保存在裝置的應用程式儲存空間。遊戲沒有玩家帳號註冊或開發者提供的雲端存檔，Android 版本已停用標準應用程式備份。我們不會透過自己的伺服器接收您的本機遊戲存檔。

年齡與隱私選擇

具備「隱私設定」的版本會詢問年齡範圍，且不預先選取答案。裝置僅儲存所選範圍或「不提供」選項，不儲存出生日期。遊戲依此設定廣告與同意保護措施。未滿 18 歲及選擇不提供年齡的玩家會採用未達同意年齡的處理方式。選擇「稍後」會維持停用廣告請求，直到完成年齡選擇。Google 可能在裝置保存同意選項，並處理提供隱私表單所需的相關資訊。

廣告與資料使用

玩家可自願觀看獎勵廣告，加速苗的成長。在具備「隱私設定」的版本中，所有廣告請求均設定為兒童導向處理、一般觀眾適用的內容等級及非個人化廣告，並在初始化廣告 SDK 前先設定年齡相關標記。遊戲在載入廣告前先檢查 Google User Messaging Platform 的同意狀態，必要時顯示 Google 同意表單。若檢查失敗，廣告載入會停用，直到重新檢查成功。完成流程後，廣告可能在玩家點擊觀看前預先載入。

Google 廣告服務

Google 廣告 SDK 可能處理 IP 位址及其推估的概略位置、應用程式和廣告互動、診斷與效能資訊，以及裝置或帳戶識別碼。實際資料依 SDK 版本與隱私設定而定。兒童導向及未達同意年齡設定會限制廣告識別碼使用及依興趣投放廣告。非個人化廣告仍可能使用資料來提供和衡量廣告及防止詐欺。Google 與其服務合作夥伴依各自政策處理資料，處理地點可能在您所在國家以外。Google 文件說明 SDK 資料傳輸使用 TLS 加密。

較早的內部測試版本

尚未具備「隱私設定」的較早內部測試版本，會在進入遊戲主畫面時初始化廣告 SDK，並可能在玩家選擇觀看前請求廣告，這些版本尚未包含上述年齡辨識及同意保護流程。安裝包含「隱私設定」的版本後，相關保護才會生效。本政策不會追溯改變舊版本的資料處理方式。

兒童與家長

遊戲預定玩家包含 6 歲以上兒童。在具備「隱私設定」的版本中，所有玩家均採兒童導向廣告處理，未滿 18 歲或未提供年齡者另採未達同意年齡的處理方式。年齡範圍保留於裝置，遊戲將對應的處理標記傳送給 Google 服務。兒童可在不觀看廣告的情況下遊玩。家長或監護人可透過聯絡信箱提出隱私、更正或刪除請求；若認為兒童曾寄送個人資訊給我們，請聯絡我們協助查找並依適用情況刪除。

客服與網站瀏覽

您寄信給我們時，我們會收到電子郵件地址及信件內容，用於處理支援與隱私請求，郵件由 Gmail 服務處理。請僅提供處理問題所需的資訊。本網站由 GitHub Pages 託管，GitHub 可能依其隱私聲明處理訪客 IP 位址與存取資訊，用於網站運作和安全。我們沒有另行加入網站分析或廣告程式。

保留、安全與刪除

本機遊戲資料與年齡偏好會保留至您清除應用程式儲存空間或解除安裝，清除時遊戲進度會一併移除。客服信件在結案後最多保留 1 個月，適用法律要求更長保留期間時依其規定辦理。Google 廣告及同意資料、GitHub 託管紀錄，依其各自政策保留；刪除本機資料不會刪除服務商已處理的資訊。我們限制客服信箱的存取，並使用服務商提供的安全保護。

您的控制與請求

您可以不觀看獎勵廣告並繼續遊玩。在具備「隱私設定」的版本中，可從「設定 → 隱私設定」閱讀政策、更新年齡範圍或開啟「廣告隱私選項」。需要 Google 隱私表單時，這個入口可用來檢視或變更選擇；無須表單時則重新檢查隱私狀態。支援的 Android 裝置另提供廣告 ID 控制。您或監護人可透過信箱就我們持有的資訊，提出查閱、更正、刪除或其他適用權利的請求。我們可能需要足以驗證和查找請求的資訊，電子郵件地址無法用來辨識裝置上的本機存檔。

政策更新

資料處理方式變更時，我們會更新本頁並標示生效日期，必要時也會在遊戲中通知重要變更。裝置上可用的隱私保護功能取決於安裝的遊戲版本。
